Our primary discipline

OSINT — Open Source Intelligence

We collect data using legal methods: public profiles, registries, leaks, metadata, infrastructure. The result is a structured report with an evidentiary base, not just a bunch of screenshots.

  • Due diligence — verification of counterparties, partners, and candidates before a deal or hire.
  • Digital deanonymization — connecting aliases, accounts, and geotags into a single cohesive profile.
  • Brand monitoring — fake profiles, phishing domains, leak of internal corporate materials.
  • Competitive intelligence — public signals about strategy, hiring, and market infrastructure.
  • Infrastructure analysis — subdomains, certificates, open ports, and services on the perimeter.
